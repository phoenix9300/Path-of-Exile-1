01Overview

A chaos poison projectile build with no weapon, from level 80 to 98.

Path of Exile 3.30 · Covens of Umbra · Softcore trade

Poisonous Concoction of Bouncing Pathfinder

No weapon, a shield, and a bottle that bounces from monster to monster poisoning everything it touches. The bottle draws on your life flasks for extra chaos damage, so Pathfinder's flask nodes are damage nodes. This guide builds the character at level 80 first, then adds four checkpoints up to level 98 whose passive trees only ever add points.

How this guide was made

I started from what real players run. On poe.ninja, 3,292 characters in the 3.30 trade league use this skill as Pathfinder (3.8% of the league when I looked). I read their items, gems and passives by level, then built the character in Path of Building Community (the development version of 14 November 2026, the first with the 3.30 tree) and let an optimiser choose the passive tree. Every number on this page comes from the same scripts.

Path of Building overrates this skill by about three times. It counts every bounce as hitting the boss and assumes every bottle has flask charges. So the optimiser does not chase Path of Building's number; it uses a model of how many bounces hit and how often the flasks have charges, explained in How it works. That model is my own and the uncertainty in it is shown wherever the damage is.

The five checkpoints

Each checkpoint is a complete Path of Building build you can import. The trees nest: every point of level 80 is in level 85, and so on up to 98, so moving up never refunds anything. What to know before you start
  • Path of Building's DPS for this skill is not comparable with other builds. Divide it by about three for a boss.
  • The damage depends on your life flasks having charges. Two big life flasks with "increased Amount Recovered" are part of the damage, not just healing.
  • Chaos resistance is 32% at level 80 and only about 47% at level 98. It is the weak spot of the build.
  • Nothing here was played. Every figure is measured in Path of Building or modelled; Unknowns and risks lists what was assumed.
Strengths
  • Every bottle bounces 13 times (15 from level 95), so packs die while you keep moving.
  • 100% spell suppression and 72% chance to evade attacks at level 80.
  • The same tree from level 80 to 98: no respec at any checkpoint.
  • It is the third most played build in the league, so items are easy to price and the build is well tested by players.

02How it works

The bottle, its flask charges, and the correction applied to Path of Building's number.

The bottle

Poisonous Concoction of Bouncing throws a bottle at a target. On impact it explodes in a small area, then bounces to another spot nearby, and keeps bouncing: 13 times at level 80 (the gem's base bounces plus projectile modifiers, which this version of the skill turns into extra bounces; that includes Fury Valve and both projectile supports). Every explosion hits and poisons. You must have no weapon; a shield is fine.

Each bottle also uses charges from a life flask, if one has enough. With charges, it adds chaos damage equal to a share of that flask's life recovery, which is why the flasks have "increased Amount Recovered" and why Pathfinder's flask notables matter. Path of Building treats a bottle as taking 8 charges.

Where Path of Building is wrong for this skill

Path of Building multiplies the skill's damage by one plus the number of bounces: it assumes every bounce hits the boss. Bounces land in random spots near the last one. Notes from players who measured it put it at about 7 or 8 of 13 bounces hitting a single big target, more in small arenas. Path of Building also assumes every bottle has flask charges. On a boss you kill nothing, so charges come only from the flask passives, the flask mastery and critical strikes, and they run out.

My estimate corrects both:

  • Bounces: half of the bounces after the first impact hit (7.5 hits per bottle at level 80, not 14).
  • Charges: 6.3 charges a second from the passives and 0.4 from critical strikes on the flask with the crit suffix, so 0.84 bottles a second have charges out of 2.71 thrown (31%). The rest use Path of Building's "No Flask" damage, which is 49% of the flask damage at level 80.

Sustain

The skill costs mana. At level 80 the bottle costs 157 mana a second at full attack speed, and mana leech returns 231 a second while you hit, so you can throw without stopping. Life leech adds 946 a second.

10Playstyle

How to play it, mapping and against bosses.

Mapping. Throw the bottle into the middle of each pack and keep moving; Shield Charge between packs. Monsters that die refill your flasks, so in maps the bottle almost always has charges. Use the Quicksilver, Silver and Jade flasks freely: Nature's Adrenaline keeps refilling them.

Bosses. Cast Despair, stand close to the boss and throw. Standing close matters: bounces land near the last impact, so a big boss next to you takes more of them. Do not drink your life flasks for healing unless you need to; their charges are your damage.

When it goes wrong. Big physical hits and degen ground. Shield Charge out, let leech and Immortal Call carry you, and come back.
