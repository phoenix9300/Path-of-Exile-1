01Overview

A fast, cheap, safe league starter built around Frost Blades and the 3.30 Warden buffs.

Path of Exile 3.30 · Covens of Umbra · Softcore trade league starter

Frost Blades Warden

Two claws, one button, and a screen that freezes before it can hit you. Frost Blades swings at the nearest monster and throws icy blades at everything behind it, so the build clears a pack in one or two swings while running. The Warden ascendancy adds a freeze that lasts at least two seconds, a tincture you can keep running almost all the time, and Avatar of the Wilds, a button worth 100% more elemental damage for 15 seconds. No unique item is required at any checkpoint.

Why this build

The brief: the most well-rounded league starter for 3.30, fast, with strong map clear. I compared the starters people are recommending for this league against what changed in the 3.30 patch notes. The 3.30 balance changes were small: six skill gems and two supports changed, and Winter Orb, one of the most played skills of 3.29, was cut. Warden was one of three ascendancies that got buffs, and two of its buffs help this build directly: Enduring Suffusion now gives 50% less mana burn (it was 30% less), and Avatar of the Wilds lasts 15 seconds instead of 10. Frost Blades Warden has been a league starter for several leagues (Zish in 3.25 to 3.28; Coolingmoon and Branarn since 3.25 and again for 3.30), so its weak points are well known.

Then I built it from scratch in Path of Building Community (the development version of 14 November 2026, the first with the 3.30 passive tree). Every passive tree here comes from an optimiser running Path of Building's own calculations, and every number on the page comes from the same scripts. Two mechanics are modelled outside Path of Building because it gets them wrong: the tincture's mana burn, and how often Avatar of the Wilds is actually up.

The three checkpoints

Each checkpoint is a full Path of Building build you can import. The trees nest: Stage 1 is part of Stage 2 and Stage 2 is part of Stage 3, so going from one to the next never refunds a point. What to know before you start
  • It is a melee build. You stand next to the first monster you hit; the blades do the rest of the pack.
  • Mana is the resource to watch. The tincture drains it and the attack costs it; leech refills it while you hit. If you stop hitting, the tincture runs dry and goes on an 8-second cooldown.
  • Chaos resistance is −60% at Stage 1. Fix it with rings and boots before red maps.
  • Nothing here was played. Every figure is measured in Path of Building or modelled; the Unknowns section lists what was assumed.
Strengths
  • Clears whole packs per swing from the first maps on.
  • Freeze stops most packs acting at all; Oath of Winter makes every freeze last at least two seconds.
  • 71% attack block from two claws plus evasion, without a shield.
  • No required uniques; every item is a rare you can craft or buy cheaply.
  • One allocation order from level 1 to Stage 3: no respec at any point.

02How it works

The four mechanics that decide the build, and where Path of Building needs a correction.

Frost Blades: one hit, then the blades

Frost Blades is a melee strike with long reach. When it hits a monster it releases ten icy blades that fly on to other monsters; the monster it hit is not hit again by its own blades. So against a single boss only the melee hit counts, and against a pack the melee hit kills one monster while the blades kill the rest. Path of Building shows the two as separate parts of the skill. This guide uses the melee hit for every boss number and the blades for every clearing number.

Physical damage becomes cold damage: 60% from the gem and 40% from the Cold mastery on the tree, so all of it is cold. Cold hits chill, and with Oath of Winter every freeze lasts at least two seconds. Hatred adds cold damage, Herald of Ice makes frozen monsters explode when they die, and Multistrike repeats each attack twice more.

Tinctures and mana burn

A tincture is a flask-slot item for melee weapons. While it is on, it adds a stack of Mana Burn every 0.7 seconds; each stack drains 1% of your maximum mana per second, so the drain speeds up the longer it runs. When your mana reaches zero the tincture switches off and every tincture goes on an 8-second cooldown. Enduring Suffusion halves the burn rate and keeps the effect for half a second per stack after it switches off, up to six seconds.

Path of Building treats the tincture as always on. I simulated it instead, using each checkpoint's own mana, mana leech and attack cost from Path of Building, while attacking without pause:

CheckpointTincture onLinger after it stopsTotal uptimeOne cycle
Stage 183%13%96%46 s
Stage 288%9%97%60 s
Stage 388%9%97%60 s

So 97% of the time the build deals what Path of Building shows; the rest of the time it deals 85% of that at Stage 3 (tincture off: 2.22M). The 24% reduced burn rate on the Stage 2 and 3 tinctures is included. Enduring Suffusion is what makes this work: the ascendancy sets without it in the design log (C and D) keep the tincture on only 57% and 71% of the time.

Avatar of the Wilds: a 100% more button, but not all the time

Avatar of the Wilds grants Unbound Avatar. You gain one Unbound Fury each time you inflict an elemental ailment with a hit, at most once every 0.2 seconds for each type of ailment. At 100 Fury you press the skill and become Unbound for 15 seconds: 100% more elemental damage, and every hit freezes, shocks and ignites. You cannot gain Fury while Unbound.

So the uptime depends on how many ailment types you inflict on the boss. This build inflicts one on pinnacle bosses, chill: they resist freeze, and cold hits do not shock or ignite. That is five Fury a second, 20 seconds to fill, then 15 seconds Unbound: up 43% of the time. A second ailment type (a shock source, for example) would cut the fill to 10 seconds and raise it to 60%; a third to 69%.

Path of Building has a checkbox, "Are you Unbound?", that doubles the damage when ticked and does nothing when not. Neither is right for a long fight. The realistic figure this guide uses weights the two by the uptime above: 3.73M at Stage 3, between the unticked 2.62M and the ticked 5.24M.

Sustain: leech pays for everything

The attack costs mana, the tincture burns it, and auras reserve most of it. What keeps it running is leech: 258 mana and 1,052 life per second while hitting at Stage 3, against an attack cost of 56 mana per second. Essence Sap (mana leech) is the first notable you take for this reason.

What I did not build

Other strong 3.30 starters were considered and not modelled here: Cyclone Slayer, Righteous Fire, Penance Brand Elementalist, Earthshatter Berserker, Lightning Arrow Deadeye and the two Pathfinder poison builds. This is a judgement, not a measurement: Earthshatter is a slow slam; Righteous Fire and Penance Brand are known as safe but slow against bosses early; Lightning Arrow is fast but has no block or freeze. Frost Blades Warden combines pack-wide clear, layered hit defence and no required uniques, and the 3.30 Warden buffs fix its old weak point, keeping the tincture running.

Every rare has at most three prefixes and three suffixes. Claws: flat physical, flat cold (and increased physical at Stage 3) as prefixes; attack speed, critical strike chance and multiplier as suffixes. The two claws are identical in the examples; in practice your off-hand can be weaker. Imperial Claw grants life per hit, which helps early.

Upgrade priority
  1. Claws: flat physical and cold damage and attack speed, on both.
  2. Life and resistances on every armour piece; chaos resistance on rings and boots before red maps.
  3. A six-link body armour (buy one linked, or use the crafting bench; the three red sockets cost extra on a Dexterity base).
  4. Spell suppression on armour until it reaches 100% with the tree.
  5. Critical strike multiplier on the amulet.

The defence is layered against hits: block, evasion and suppression, with freeze stopping most attackers outright. It is weak against damage over time and against big physical hits that land: the biggest physical hit you survive at Stage 3 is about 4,565.

Mother's Teachings grants Barkskin: up to 10 bark, gained two a second while you are not hit. Each bark reduces physical damage from attack hits; each missing bark adds evasion instead. Path of Building rates Stage 1 higher with full bark, but Stages 2 and 3 about 15% higher with no bark (their evasion is higher), so both columns are shown.

Stage 1 has no chaos resistance at all (-60%). That is normal for a league start but is the first thing to fix. Steelskin adds about 4% EHP at Stage 3.

09Playstyle

How to play it, mapping and against bosses.

Mapping. Hold Frost Blades on the nearest monster and keep moving with Whirling Blades. Turn the tincture on when you enter the map and leave it alone; if it runs dry, keep attacking and it comes back in eight seconds. Recast Blood Rage when its charges run out. Use the Quicksilver and Silver flasks on cooldown.

Bosses. Cast Frostbite on the boss, throw Frost Bomb, make sure the tincture is on, and attack. Press Avatar of the Wilds whenever the Fury meter is full. Frostblink with Bonechill on top of the boss when you need to reposition.

When it goes wrong. Most deaths on this kind of build come from a big physical hit or from standing in a degen. Leave the fight, let bark build up (it grows while you are not hit), then come back.
